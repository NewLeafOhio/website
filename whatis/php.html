What’s a Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP) for Addiction Treatment?

A Partial Hospitalization Program (PHP) offers intensive addiction treatment for those who need more support than outpatient therapy without requiring overnight care. PHP provides a structured, daily approach to recovery, serving as a step between inpatient care and traditional outpatient programs.

What You Can Expect from PHP:

    Daily Support:
  • Daily Support: You’ll attend the center 4-6 hours a day, several days a week, offering structure while allowing you to return home each night.
  • Comprehensive Therapy: YPHP includes individual and group therapy, family counseling, and skill-building sessions on managing stress and preventing relapse.
  • Expert Care Team: A team of doctors, therapists, and addiction specialists supports both your mental and physical health.
  • Focus on Long-Term Sobriety: PHP is ideal if you need daily support but don’t require 24-hour supervision. It’s a strong option after inpatient treatment or if traditional outpatient therapy isn’t enough.
  • Holistic Approach: Integration of therapy with skill-building activities and support groups.
