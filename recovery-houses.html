Recovery Houses in Cuyahoga County

A safe, substance-free place to live can make early recovery much easier. Search recovery houses across Cleveland and Cuyahoga County, then call the ones that fit to ask about openings.

Call to confirm availability. Openings change daily, and this list does not show which houses have an empty bed right now. Every house sets its own rules, costs, and intake process, so ask about those when you call.

Peer-run: residents run the house together. Managed: a house manager oversees the home. Supervised: staff are on site with more structure. Houses that did not list who they serve are hidden when you pick a group above.

Where this list comes from. Houses come from the Ohio Department of Behavioral Health's public recovery housing registry (Cuyahoga County report dated September 17, 2026). To report a correction or a missing house, email info@newleafohio.com. You can also use the state's own recovery housing search.
