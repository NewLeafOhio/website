Whether you are looking for treatment, a place to live, or a meeting tonight, these are trusted places to start.

If you are in danger right now, call 911. For emotional crisis, call or text 988. For substance use help, call 1-800-662-HELP (4357). Both are free and open 24/7.

Which level of care is right?

A good program starts with an assessment. In general:

  • Medical detox: medical supervision while your body clears alcohol or drugs, when withdrawal could be dangerous.
  • Residential: you live at the program for a period while you get treatment.
  • Partial hospitalization (PHP): full days of treatment several days a week, while you live at home or in recovery housing.
  • Intensive outpatient (IOP): several hours of treatment a few days a week, so you can keep up with work, school, or family.
  • Counseling and peer support: ongoing one-on-one and group support as you build a life in recovery.

Not sure where you fit? Call us and we will help you figure it out, even if the answer is a different program.

Choosing a program safely

Some websites and phone numbers that look like treatment centers are really referral call centers that are paid to steer you to certain facilities. Before you commit:

  • Ask, "Are you the treatment provider, or do you refer people elsewhere?"
  • Ask for the program's name and address, and confirm it is licensed or certified with the Ohio Department of Behavioral Health.
  • Be careful with offers of free travel, cash, or gifts to choose a program.
  • Check your insurance yourself by calling the number on your card.
